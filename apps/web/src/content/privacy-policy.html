Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gulgle Project ("Gulgle", "we", or "us") handles information when you use the Gulgle browser extension, website, and sync service.

At a glance

  • Gulgle does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.
  • Signing in is optional and is only used to provide account and settings-sync features.
  • Search routing happens in your browser. Gulgle does not send search queries to its sync service or store them in your Gulgle account.

Information Gulgle handles

Search queries

Gulgle processes your search query on your device to select a bang or default search provider and construct the destination URL. The query is not sent to the Gulgle sync service or saved in your Gulgle account. When you use the hosted search URL, the request URL may be processed by our web host, GitHub Pages. When Gulgle redirects you, the destination search provider or website receives the query or URL and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Settings stored on your device

Gulgle stores your custom bangs, default search provider, theme, and sync timestamp in local browser or extension storage. Custom bangs can include the trigger, name, destination URL, domain, and aliases you enter. This information stays on your device unless you sign in and use settings sync.

GitHub account information

If you choose to sign in, Gulgle uses GitHub OAuth and requests the GitHub user scope. Gulgle uses the resulting access only to retrieve your GitHub numeric account identifier, display name, and profile email address if GitHub returns one. We use this information to create and display your Gulgle account and associate it with your synced settings. Gulgle does not receive your GitHub password, access your repositories, or modify your GitHub profile. The GitHub access token used to retrieve this profile information is not stored by Gulgle after sign-in.

Synced settings and authentication data

When you sign in, Gulgle stores a copy of your custom bangs, default search provider, and last-modified timestamp on the Gulgle sync service so the settings can be used across your devices. Gulgle also handles short-lived access credentials, hashed session credentials, and related security records to keep you signed in and protect your account.

Technical information

Like most online services, Gulgle and its hosting providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser or device type, requested path, and request time to deliver and secure the service and diagnose failures. Gulgle does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How Gulgle uses information

Gulgle uses the information described above only to:

  • route searches to the destination you choose;
  • authenticate you and maintain your session;
  • store and synchronize your settings across devices;
  • operate, troubleshoot, and secure Gulgle; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our rights.

When information is shared

Gulgle may disclose information only in the following circumstances:

  • GitHub. GitHub provides OAuth authentication and GitHub Pages web hosting. The Gulgle website also requests the repository's public star count from GitHub. GitHub receives the information required to provide those services, including standard request information. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Search and destination providers. When Gulgle sends your browser to a provider or website you select, that destination receives the query or URL needed to complete your request.
  • Infrastructure providers. Service providers that host the Gulgle sync server or database may process information only as needed to operate those services on our behalf.
  • Legal and security reasons. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect Gulgle's rights and security.
  • Change of control. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this policy and applicable law.

Gulgle does not sell or rent personal information.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Information obtained through the Gulgle browser extension is used only to provide or improve Gulgle's user-facing features. It is not used for personalized advertising or transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve Gulgle, comply with applicable law, address security or abuse, or complete a change-of-control transaction. People do not read extension user data unless you give affirmative permission for a specific purpose, it is necessary for security or legal compliance, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations. Gulgle's handling of extension user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention, and security

Local settings remain on your device until you clear the relevant browser or extension data or uninstall the extension. Account information and synced settings remain until you delete your account. You can do that from Settings > Account > Delete account; deletion removes your Gulgle identity, cloud settings, and server-side sessions. Gulgle access tokens expire after 15 minutes, and server-side session records expire after no more than 30 days. Limited operational or security records and backups may remain for a reasonable period when required for security, recovery, or legal obligations.

Gulgle uses safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network transport in production, restricted session cookies, short-lived access credentials, and hashing server-side refresh credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

  • You can use Gulgle without signing in.
  • You can clear locally stored information through your browser or extension settings.
  • You can delete your Gulgle account and synced data from Gulgle's account settings.
  • You can revoke Gulgle's access from your GitHub account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Gulgle's practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the product or repository when appropriate.
